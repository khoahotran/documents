# Career

Personal career-development material — distinct from the technical subject trees elsewhere in the repository.

## Subfolders

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `interview-prep/` | General and role-specific interview question banks (Vietnamese general-interview guide, NestJS, JavaScript, full-stack). Note: `Javascript question.pdf` and `FULL STACK DEVELOPER_INTERVIEW QUESTIONS MASTER.pdf` are scanned/image-only PDFs with no extractable text — flagged `NEEDS_REVIEW`, their exact content isn't confirmed beyond the filename. |
| `job-search/` | Vietnamese email templates covering the full job-application lifecycle: apply → interview confirmation/decline → offer negotiation → offer acceptance/decline → start-date confirmation. |
| `it-career-guidance/` | A self-published Vietnamese IT-career guidebook (IT fundamentals + career guidance + 30 years of personal reflections). |
| `professional-skills/` | Two general communication-skills guides (a self-introduction framework, and a "how to prepare a presentation" guide) — broader than interview-prep specifically, which is why they're split out rather than filed under `interview-prep/`. |
